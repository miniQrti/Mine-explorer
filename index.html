Keyboard & navigation

Tab into the board, then use arrow keys to move between visible tiles. Enter or Space reveals a tile; F places or removes a flag. Drag to pan, pinch or use + / − to zoom. The crosshair centers explored ground; the entrance button returns your view to the gold ENTRY tile.

 READ THE MINE

Symbol legend

Your starting point is the gold ENTRY tile. There is no character to move—explore outward by revealing covered tiles along the edge of explored ground.

  • Entrance · ENTRY

    The safe place where this floor begins. The entrance control returns your view here.

  • Stairs down

    Reveal these to descend to the next floor.

  • 2
    Number clue

    Mines in the eight surrounding tiles, including diagonals.

  • Empty floor

    No neighboring mines. Connected empty ground opens automatically.

  • ◆
    Covered tile

    An unknown tile beside explored ground. You can reveal it or flag it.

  • Flag

    Your note for a suspected mine. Right-click or hold to place or remove it.

  • Solid rock

    Unbreakable terrain. It cannot contain mines or be explored.

  • Fog of war

    Unexplored darkness. Advance from revealed ground to uncover its edge.

  • Mine

    Hitting one ends this attempt. Your selected mode decides which floor restarts.
